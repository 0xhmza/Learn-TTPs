## What is this?

The cyber threat landscape never sleeps and neither do the adversaries in it. Every day, attackers move through networks using techniques that have names, IDs, and documented patterns. **Learn TTPs** puts that entire playbook in your hands and trains you to recognize it on sight.

No accounts. No subscriptions. No fluff. Just you, your brain, and **700+ real-world attack techniques** from the MITRE ATT&CK framework.



## Why it works

Most people *read* about TTPs once and forget them by next Tuesday. That's not how expertise is built.

Learn TTPs uses **spaced repetition** — the same science-backed method used by medical students and language learners to show you each card at the exact moment before you'd forget it. A few weeks in, and technique names like `T1059.001 · PowerShell` or `T1078 · Valid Accounts` stop being trivia. They become reflexes. You start *thinking* in TTPs.

> *Knowing the attacker's language fluently means faster detections, sharper threat models, and conversations that actually move the needle.*



## Two ways to train

### 🃏 Flashcards
Flip through techniques and mitigations. The app tracks exactly what you know, what you're shaky on, and what you've nailed. Then surfaces the right card at the right moment using the **SM-2 algorithm** (same engine behind Anki).

### 🎯 Guess the Mitigation
An attack technique is shown. You pick the defenses that stop it. No hints, no hand-holding! Pure applied knowledge.
